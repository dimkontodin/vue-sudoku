Sudoku – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Sudoku (the Android app, the web app and the desktop app, together “the app”) is a free, open-source game by Dimitris Kontodinas. It works entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. It does not send any data off your device and does not request network access on Android.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics and settings are stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). They never leave your device, except through your operating system's own backup feature if you have it enabled. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Camera and photos

If you import a puzzle from a photo, the image is processed on your device only. It is not uploaded or stored by the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
